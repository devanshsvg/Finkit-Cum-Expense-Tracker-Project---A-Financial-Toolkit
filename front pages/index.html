FinKit — Your Personal Finance & Investment Management Platform

Your Finance Bestfriend

FinKit is a full-stack personal finance management platform designed to help users take control of their money and make smarter financial decisions. Users can track their income and expenses, manage budgets, monitor savings goals, and visualize their financial activity through interactive dashboards and reports. The platform allows users to organize transactions into categories, with support for both manual categorization and smart category suggestions.
